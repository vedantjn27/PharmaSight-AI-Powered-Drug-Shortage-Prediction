   1. GET  /health                           - Health check
   2. GET  /api/v1/drugs                     - Drug data
   3. POST /api/v1/upload                    - CSV + TFT inference (PRIMARY)
   4. GET  /api/v1/forecast/{drug}           - 30-day forecast

   9. GET  /api/v1/purchase_orders           - Purchase recommendations
